Supporting Alconbury Robotics

How you can help our team succeed and inspire

What you are supporting

Your supporting one of five FRC teams in the UK, and the first community FRC team in the UK. Your support will help us achieve our goals and inspire our community, region and people around the world.

Your support, whether it be in-kind donations, money, credit, materials, or any kind of support, will go to helping our team succeed in this season, running outreach events and donating and helping local schools and other robotics teams. Your support will help us achieve our goals.

Sponsoring our team

Our team is a non-profit organization that relies on the support of sponsors to fund our journey and outreach goals. We are always looking for new sponsors to help us achieve our goals and inspire the next generation of engineers and innovators. If you are interested in sponsoring our team, please contact us at frc11784@gmail.com.

We have charitable status through fiscal sponsorship from CamCare, and 501(c)(3) tax-deductible donations from Hack Club Bank. If you are interested in donating to our team, we recommend checking out our sponsorship packet for more details on the benefits you get from donating to our team.

How to donate


You have multiple options for donating to our team. You can donate by:

  • Donating through the FIRST Dashboard, provides a streamlines process for us and can use FIRST's 501(c)(3) status for tax deductions. Good for US Companies, but more complex to donate through
  • Donating through Hack Club Bank, we will lose 7% of the donation but you can use Hack Club's 501(c)(3) status for tax deductions. Good for US companies, and easier to donate through
  • Writing a check directly to our team. This is a simple way to donate, but it may take longer for the donation to be processed. We do not directly have an organization to handle checks.
  • Donating through CamCare, useful for UK donors to use their HMRC Charitable status for lowering your taxable income (read more about how to reduce your taxable income with this method)
